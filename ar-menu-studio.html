How do I get a 3D scan of a dish?
  1. Scan the plated dish with a phone-based 3D capture app — Polycam, KIRI Engine, or Luma AI all work well for small objects and food.
  2. Export the result as .glb (and .usdz too, if the app offers it — that's what lets it open natively in AR on iPhone).
  3. Upload those files somewhere with a permanent public link: your own website, Cloudflare R2, Firebase Storage, or even a GitHub repo.
  4. Paste that link into "Hosted link" on the left, then generate the QR on the right.

2 · Get your QR

Your QR code will appear here, styled as a ticket stub — ready to place next to the dish's name on the printed menu.
